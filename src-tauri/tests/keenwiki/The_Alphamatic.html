The Alphamatic is a Keen 5 mod released on the Fourth of July 2017 by Gridlock. This mod is an Atroxian Realm sequel. Version 2.0 with four extra secret levels titled "Marooned Over Mars Expansion" was released on Keen Day 2021. Additionally, on Keen Day 2026, Gridlock published version 3.0 which introduced the bonus secret level Binary Circuits and several level design changes.

Story

The story continues where Atroxian Realm left off. Keen faces a bigger and even deadlier Armageddon Machine as seen in Keen 5. This time he must save our Solar System from VitaCorp and unravel the mystery of the Atroxians.

Actors

All actors from the original game return, except for Slicestars and Spirogrips, the former of which are replaced, as shown below, and the latter of which were recycled to make room for extra QED frames.
