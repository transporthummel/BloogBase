Candy Dreams was the first attempt to mod Keen Dreams made by Levellass. The project was officially abandoned in October 2009, and released in alpha state. Sadly the seemingly much more advanced beta state of the project has been lost. According to Levellass the project was shut down due to difficulties in editing levels.

Story

Billy Blaze, 8 year old genius who has constructed an intergalactic spaceship in his backyard clubhouse, defeated the Vorticon fleet and freed them from the Grand Intellect, single handedly saved the galaxy, ransacked the dreamland of Tuberia (twice!) to destroy the dream machine, and has just returned from saving the universe from his arch enemy Mortimer McMire, is fighting to stay conscious.

Despite his aforementioned arch enemy sitting behind him shooting spitballs at him, Billy just can't keep his eyes open. The guest speakers lecture is just so dull, even Billy's love of school (Or rather academic things) can keep him awake.

As the lecturer (A visiting pyrotechnician) describes how to make a really cool homemade exploding candle, Keen slips into sleep.

Just before he loses consciousness, he can almost hear a snicker...
